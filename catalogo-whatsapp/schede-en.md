### Percentage on what is recovered

**Prezzo nel catalogo:** Free

12 per cent on the sums actually recovered, calculated net of the bank charges and taxes paid to obtain the recovery. Only on money actually credited to you, within 24 months of signing. If nothing comes back, you owe us nothing.

**How it is calculated** 12 % on the sums actually recovered by you, net of the bank charges and taxes paid to obtain the recovery.

**When it applies** Only on money actually credited, within 24 months of signing the contract.
**When it does not** On amounts merely «recognised» in your favour, and on the initial fee.
**If nothing comes back** You owe us nothing.

Example: on a 20,000 EUR case where 6,000 EUR comes back, the percentage is 720 EUR, on top of the 190 EUR fee.

### Guide: what to do in the first 48 hours

**Prezzo nel catalogo:** Free

The first six things to do after a fraud, in the right order: stop the payments without closing the account, secure the wallet, keep the evidence, file the complaint, ask for data preservation, report to the authorities. Free.

**The order matters more than the speed**
1. Stop the payments, but do not close the account: the statement is the main evidence.
2. If you gave access to a wallet, treat it as compromised: create a new one and move the remaining tokens.
3. Keep everything now: screenshots, emails, chats, receipts, transaction hashes.
4. File the complaint: any Carabinieri station or police station, or online with the State Police.
5. Ask your bank and the exchange to preserve the data: retention periods are limited, every day counts.
6. Report it to the supervisory authorities: CONSOB, AGCM, OCLAFP.

**Cost** None. The full guide is on the site, on the Support page.
