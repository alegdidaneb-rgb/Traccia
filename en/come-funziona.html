The method

From your account to a document you can use.

We do not promise to bring your money back: nobody can. We undertake to tell you, with technical evidence, where it went and whether there is a viable route. If there is not, we say so.

01

Listening and collection

A call of about thirty minutes, by phone or video, then the collection of documents. In this phase we establish whether the case falls within our remit: online financial fraud, investments, crypto, fake asset recovery.

What we need

Transfers, receipts, screenshots of the platform, emails and chats with whoever contacted you.

Time

1–2 days from the first report.

Cost

None. The initial assessment is free.

02

Tracing the flow

We follow the money. With transfers we reconstruct the chain of destination accounts and their holders; with crypto we follow the transactions on the blockchain, identifying exchanges, clustering wallets and mixing services. This is the decisive phase: if no trace remains here, the rest makes no sense.

What we produce

Flow diagram, list of addresses and institutions involved, timeline.

Time

3–5 working days.

Possible outcome

We stop here, at no cost, if nothing actionable emerges.

03

Expert report

A dated, signed and numbered document that describes the path of the funds, backed by the data collected. It is our product: it helps you understand, and it helps the lawyer build the filing.

Contents

Timeline, addresses and hashes, institutions involved, points of attachment, limits of the analysis.

Time

10 working days from complete delivery of the documents.

Cost

Initial fee of €190 including VAT, charged once.

04

Complaints and reports

We help you prepare the filings you can submit yourself: a criminal complaint, a report to the Postal Police, to the AGCM, to the OCLAFP, and requests to banks or exchanges for data preservation and freezing of the sums.

Who signs

You, for the filings that concern you. A lawyer, for those that require one.

Time

2–4 weeks to prepare.

Cost

Included in the initial fee; any lawyer's fees are separate.

05

Coordination with the lawyer

If the case holds up, the report goes to your lawyer or to one of the professionals we work with. We stay available for technical clarifications, supplements and cross-examination. We are not the ones defending you in court.

Who pays the lawyer

You, directly, according to their quote. We take no percentage of their fees.

Time

They do not depend on us: months or years, depending on the type of action.

If the conditions are not there

We tell you and close the case at no further cost.

The document

What an expert report contains.

It is not a report generated by software. It is a document written by a person, who answers for what they write.

Every report also states the limits of the analysis: what could not be verified, which data was missing, which conclusions remain hypotheses. A document that admits no limits is not an expert report.

Standard structure

  1. Engagement and subject of the analysis
  2. Documents examined and their origin
  3. Timeline of transfers, with dates and amounts
  4. Blockchain addresses, hashes and clusters
  5. Institutions, exchanges and parties involved
  6. Points of attachment: where action is still possible
  7. Limits, missing data and conclusions
  8. Technical annexes and signature
To be clear

Things worth clarifying straight away.

Is the initial fee an advance on the refund?

No. It is the price of the analysis and the document, paid once. It is not linked to the outcome and is not refundable, because the work is done regardless. The percentage applies only to what is actually recovered.

If the case goes nowhere, will you ask me for more money?

No. If phase 2 finds nothing, we stop and do not charge the fee. If the report has already been produced and conditions to proceed then fall away, the case ends there: no extra cost, no renewal.

Do I have to give the credentials of my account or wallet?

Never. Statements, transfer receipts and screenshots are enough. If a wallet is still under your control, we do not need to enter it: we explain what to do without asking for the seed phrase. Anyone who asks you for it, whoever they are, is stealing it.

Do you only work on Italian frauds?

We take cases involving Italian institutions or tax residents in Italy. If the money has left for jurisdictions that do not cooperate, analysis is still possible but the chances of recovery drop sharply, and we say so in the initial assessment.

Can I come to you months after the fact?

Yes, but time works against it: institutions keep data for limited periods and funds move. If you have been defrauded, the most useful thing to do right away is to preserve everything and report it; the analysis can come later.
