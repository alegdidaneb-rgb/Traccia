Legal notice

Who we are, what we do, what we are not, and what we cannot promise.

Last updated: 30 October 2026

1. Owner of the site

The site and the «Traccia» brand are owned by ERSEL SPA, with registered office and head office at Piazza Solferino, 11 - 10121 Turin (TO) — VAT and tax code 06900230018.

2. Nature of the service

Traccia carries out technical and documentary analysis of financial flows linked to alleged frauds, and assists the user in preparing reports and complaints.

Traccia is not:

  • a law firm or a lawyer: legal documents are signed by qualified professionals;
  • a credit institution, financial intermediary or payment service provider;
  • a debt collection company authorised under Art. 115 of the Italian Public Security Act (TULPS);
  • a private investigation agency;
  • a party that negotiates settlements with the perpetrators of a fraud on the user's behalf.

3. No guarantee of recovery

Traccia does not in any way or at any stage guarantee recovery of the sums reported. The percentages, average times and case data published describe our work and are neither a forecast nor a promise of outcome.

The outcome depends on factors beyond our control: traceability of the funds, destination jurisdiction, cooperation of intermediaries, the pace of the courts.

4. Anti-fraud warning

Traccia never contacts first anyone who has not filed a report, never makes cold calls, never buys lists of defrauded people and never operates through social media messages.

Traccia never asks for and never accepts: payments in cryptocurrency, advances to «unlock» refunds, advance taxes, remote access to the user's device, banking credentials, OTP codes, private keys or seed phrases. Anyone making such requests, even in the name of Traccia, should be reported to assistenza@traccia.it.com.

5. Informational content

The content of the site, including the pages on fraud warning signs and on cases, is informational and educational. It is not legal, tax or financial advice, nor an invitation to invest.

The cryptocurrency prices published are provided by public third-party sources, are indicative and may be subject to delays or inaccuracies. Traccia does not offer services for buying or selling financial instruments or crypto-assets.

6. Published cases

The cases described on the site are published anonymously, with rounded amounts and without identifying references to the people, companies or institutions involved. They are reconstructions for explanation and not court records.

7. Company data and authorisations

ERSEL SPA is not a bank, is not a financial intermediary and is not entered in the register referred to in Art. 106 of Italian Legislative Decree 385/1993 (Consolidated Banking Act). It does not carry out investment services or collective asset management and is not supervised by the Bank of Italy or CONSOB. The activity carried out — technical and documentary analysis of financial flows and assistance in preparing complaints — is not among those reserved by law. The company information on the site is that resulting from public registers as at the date of the last update. If another source reports an entry in the Register of Banks or a register number for ERSEL SPA that differs from what is stated here, this notice prevails.

8. External links

The site may contain links to third-party sites (supervisory authorities, police forces, news sources). Traccia does not control those contents and is not responsible for their accuracy or availability.

9. Reports and complaints

For reports about the site or the conduct of the company, write to assistenza@traccia.it.com. We reply within 48 working hours. The procedure is described in the privacy page.
