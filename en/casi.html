In detail

How the outcome came about.

Traced T-2418 · 2026

Fake CFD broker, €38,400

How it started
Sponsored ad on a social network, then contact on WhatsApp with a «consultant» showing steady returns of 3 % a month.
Mechanism
Four transfers to two IBANs of foreign companies, converted into USDT on an intermediary exchange.
Outcome of the analysis
Two points of attachment: an exchange with full client identification and an account held at an EU institution.
Where we got to
Report in 9 days, data preservation request, case handed to a lawyer for civil action. The proceedings are still ongoing.
Partial T-1972 · 2025

Social trading, €12,400, of which about €4,100 recovered

How it started
A Telegram «signal» group with screenshots of profits and a fake demo account that looked real.
Mechanism
Transfers to an Italian account opened by a shell company, then cash withdrawals at the counter.
Outcome of the analysis
Part of the money was still in the account when the report came in: it was blocked at the institution's request and returned after the procedure.
Where we got to
About a third recovered. The rest had already been withdrawn in cash and is no longer traceable. We closed the case saying there was nothing more to be done.
No trace T-2033 · 2025

Fake recovery service, €6,900

How it started
The client had already lost money on a fake platform. Then he was contacted by a «debt recovery» outfit asking for €1,200 in advance to release the funds.
Mechanism
Crypto payment to a personal wallet, moved at once through a mixing service.
Outcome of the analysis
No point of attachment. The funds left the traceable perimeter before the case reached us.
Where we got to
Case closed at phase 2 without charging the fee. We gave the client the documentation for the complaint and the contacts for reporting it.
Traced T-2301 · 2025

Fake staking platform, €22,000

How it started
A platform whose domain had been registered three months earlier, promising 18 % a year on stablecoins and showing an invented team.
Mechanism
Deposits in ETH and USDT to addresses generated per client, later consolidated into three clustering wallets.
Outcome of the analysis
The clustering wallets were linked to an exchange subject to identification duties. Two addresses were still active at the time of the analysis.
Where we got to
Report delivered, reports to the OCLAFP and the Postal Police, freeze request filed through the lawyer. Outcome pending.

Note on the data. The cases are published anonymously, with rounded amounts and internal references. We do not publish the names of the people, companies or institutions involved, nor details that could identify them. «Traced» means that identifiable parties exist to act against: it does not mean the money has been recovered.

We cannot guarantee that funds will be recovered: the outcome depends on factors beyond our control, such as whether the funds can be traced, cooperation from the institutions involved and the pace of the courts.
