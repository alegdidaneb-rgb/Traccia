Terms and conditions

General conditions governing the technical and documentary analysis service. The contract signed for each individual case prevails over these conditions on economic matters.

Last updated: 30 October 2026

1. Subject

ERSEL SPA (hereinafter «Traccia») provides a service of technical and documentary analysis aimed at reconstructing the path of sums of money the user paid to third parties in the context of alleged financial frauds, and at assisting in the preparation of reports and criminal complaints.

The service is technical and documentary in nature. It does not constitute legal advice, investment advice, financial intermediation, debt collection on behalf of third parties or private investigation.

2. Nature of the service and absence of any guarantee

Traccia does not guarantee the recovery of the sums reported. The outcome depends on factors beyond Traccia's control, including the traceability of the funds, the cooperation of the intermediaries involved, the pace of the judicial authorities and the destination jurisdiction of the funds.

The user acknowledges being aware that no operator can legitimately promise a recovery outcome.

3. Access to the service

The first assessment is free. Access to the client area requires creating an account with personal credentials. The user must keep the credentials confidential and must not share them.

Traccia may refuse or end the engagement if the documents show the case falls outside the scope of the service, or if grounds for a conflict of interest exist.

4. Obligations of the user

The user undertakes to:

  • provide truthful, complete and unaltered documentation;
  • not to give banking credentials, OTP codes, private keys or seed phrases to anyone, including parties presenting themselves as appointed by Traccia;
  • not install remote access software at the request of third parties in the context of the case;
  • inform Traccia of any contact received from other parties offering recovery services;
  • cooperate promptly with requests for additional documents.

5. Fees and payments

The service provides for an initial fee of €190 including VAT, due once and not linked to the outcome of the analysis, and a percentage of 12 % applied exclusively to the sums actually recovered by the user, calculated net of the bank charges and taxes borne for the recovery.

The initial fee is paid only after the free assessment and the signing of the engagement contract; it is not due when the analysis is stopped at the reconstruction phase for lack of actionable elements.

Payments are made exclusively by transfer to the IBAN held in the name of ERSEL SPA and stated in the contract. Payments in cryptocurrency, by prepaid card or to accounts held by individuals other than the legal representative are not accepted. Every payment is documented by a proper invoice.

6. Items not included

Traccia's fees do not include: the fees of any lawyer appointed by the user, court fees and unified contributions, translation, legalisation and service-abroad costs, and the charges applied by banks, exchanges and other intermediaries. These items are paid by the user directly to the respective providers.

7. Withdrawal

The user may withdraw at any time, without penalty, by giving written notice to assistenza@traccia.it.com. Withdrawal before the analysis begins involves no cost. Later withdrawal means losing the initial fee, in respect of the work already done, and stopping the activity at once, with no further amounts due.

Traccia may withdraw with written notice, returning the part of the initial fee not yet justified by the work done, should technical or professional reasons make it impossible to continue.

8. Limitation of liability

Traccia is liable for performing the engagement with professional diligence. To the maximum extent permitted by law, Traccia's total liability for the engagement does not exceed the fees actually paid by the user for that case.

Traccia is not liable for the outcomes of actions taken by intermediaries, judicial authorities or third parties, nor for damage arising from inaccurate or incomplete information provided by the user.

9. Data processing

The processing of personal data, including the documents uploaded to the client area, is governed by theprivacy notice and takes place solely for the purpose of performing the engagement and complying with legal obligations.

10. Intellectual property

The report and the documents produced are intended for the personal use of the user and of the professionals they appoint. They may not be published, resold or used for commercial purposes without written authorisation.

11. Governing law and jurisdiction

These conditions are governed by Italian law. For any dispute, the court of the consumer's place of residence or elected domicile has jurisdiction where the consumer is entitled to it under the Italian Consumer Code; otherwise, the court of Turin.
