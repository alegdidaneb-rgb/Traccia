Necessary notice. Traccia carries out technical and documentary analysis and assists in reconstructing financial flows. We are not a lawyer, a law firm, a credit institution, a debt collection company authorised under art. 115 of the Italian Public Security Act (TULPS), nor a financial intermediary. We cannot guarantee that funds will be recovered: the outcome depends on factors beyond our control, such as whether the funds can be traced, cooperation from the institutions involved and the pace of the courts. No operator can promise a guaranteed refund: anyone who does is running a scam within a scam. Our WhatsApp number is only the one published on this site: we never write first and never ask for money in chat.

Anti-fraud. We never ask you to pay money up front to «unlock» a refund, to pay advance taxes, to install remote-access software or to share OTP codes and private keys. Any request of this kind should be reported immediately to assistenza@traccia.it.com.
