Support

Urgent things first, then the rest.

If you have just been defrauded, the order of the steps matters more than how quickly you write to us. Here is what to do in the first hours, what to keep and where to report it.

Now

The first six things to do.

In the order they should be done.

1

Stop the payments, do not close the account

Cancel recurring charges and direct debits. Contact your bank to stop further transfers to that IBAN, but do not close the account: the statement is the main evidence, and the complaint needs it.

2

If you gave access to the wallet, make it safe

If you shared a seed phrase or signed an approval transaction, the wallet must be treated as compromised: create a new one and move the remaining tokens. What has already left cannot be recovered, but the rest is saved.

3

Keep everything, now

Screenshots of the pages, emails, chats, social profiles, transfer receipts, transaction hashes. Save them somewhere other than your computer too: platforms shut down their sites and delete their channels within weeks.

4

Complaint

You can file a complaint at any Carabinieri station or police station, or online with the State Police. For online fraud the Postal Police is most often responsible. You need the documents collected in point 3.

5

Ask for data preservation

Write to the bank and the exchange involved asking them to preserve the data on the transactions and their recipients. Traffic data and counterparty information are kept for limited periods: every day counts.

6

Report it to the supervisory authorities

To CONSOB if the platform offers investment services without authorisation, to the AGCM for unfair commercial practices, to the OCLAFP for suspicious transaction reports. These are reports distinct from the criminal complaint and serve to stop the platform, not to get your money back.

Second fraud

«A payment is needed to unlock the funds»: no.

It is the most widespread scheme and it targets people who have already been defrauded once, even months later. It works because it arrives when you would do anything to get the money back.

That applies to us too: our fee is the price of a technical analysis, it is paid once, and it does not depend on the outcome. We have no «unlocking fee» and have never asked for an advance in cryptocurrency.

Requests that are always a fraud

  • ✕ Advance tax to be paid to «release» the capital
  • ✕ Payment in cryptocurrency to a personal wallet
  • ✕ Remote access to your computer with control software
  • ✕ OTP codes, PINs or account credentials
  • ✕ Seed phrase of the wallet, «to verify ownership»
  • ✕ Contacts you never sought who call you offering a recovery

The principle

Everything that has already happened leaves a trace somewhere: in your bank, on your phone, in the public blockchain ledgers. The work is putting the pieces together before they expire.

That is why collection must be done at once, even if you later decide not to go ahead with anyone.

Intake

Who to turn to in Italy.

They are public and free channels. None of them recovers your money: they serve to stop the fraud and to build the evidence.

Postal Police

For online fraud. You can file the complaint at an office or through the online police station.

Carabinieri or State Police

Any station is competent to take a complaint. Ask for a copy with the reference number.

CONSOB

To report parties offering investment services without authorisation. It keeps a list of warnings on abusive platforms: worth checking before investing.

Bank of Italy

To report irregular conduct by authorised intermediaries and for matters concerning accounts and payments.

AGCM

For unfair commercial practices and misleading advertising by platforms.

OCLAFP — Bank of Italy

Receives reports of suspicious transactions. Access is normally through an intermediary, but it is useful to know it exists.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

I have lost everything, I cannot even afford the analysis

The initial assessment is free, and the first thing to do is the criminal complaint, which costs nothing. If the case goes nowhere, we do not charge the fee. If it does and money is tight, tell us: we assess case by case whether and how to proceed.

I paid by card: can I do a chargeback?

You can ask your bank or the card issuer, and in some cases the deadlines are very tight (often 120 days from the transaction). It is not guaranteed: it depends on the type of payment and the scheme's rules. It is nevertheless one of the first things to try, and it must be done at once.

How long do I have to act?

There is no single deadline, but three matter: the chargeback periods (months), data retention by intermediaries (months) and the limitation periods for civil actions (years). The first two run out first.

Should I tell my family?

Shame is the fraudster's main lever, and the reason many frauds go unreported. Talking about it helps: it reduces the risk that the second fraud works, which is what most often happens.

Can I report a platform to you without opening a case?

Yes, by writing to assistenza@traccia.it.com. Reports help us recognise recurring patterns. We cannot, however, give legal opinions to people who are not our clients.
