The five stages, in detail
Where we work

Every scam leaves a different trace.

The method is the same; what changes is where we look. We work on the five patterns we see most often, with different tools and counterparties for each.

Fake brokers and trading platforms

Cloned websites, «advisers» on chat, guaranteed returns. We start from the transfers to foreign IBANs.

Crypto scams

Fake exchanges, non-existent staking, emptied wallets. The public ledger is our main source.

Fake recovery services

Those who promise a refund against an advance. They are often the same people behind the first scam.

Payment and card fraud

Unauthorised charges, bank phishing, chargebacks to open within tight deadlines.

«Alternative» income and investments

Property, forests, receivables, gold: fixed-return promises with no product underneath.

What we do not do
and what nobody should promise you.

The «fund recovery» industry is full of people profiting from despair. These are the limits we set for ourselves, written into the contract and not only on the website.

Read the limits of the service
  • ✕ We do not guarantee recovery. Anyone who guarantees it is selling hope, not a service.
  • ✕ We do not ask for advances to «unlock» a refund. The initial fee covers the analysis, and it is paid once.
  • ✕ We never ask for private keys, seed phrases or remote access to your computer or your online banking.
  • ✕ We do not hire hackers. There are no «ethical hackers» who recover wallets for a fee: that is a second scam.
  • ✕ We do not promise fixed timescales. The Italian courts have their own, and we do not set them.
Questions

The questions everyone asks us.

What does it cost to try?

The first assessment is free: we read the documentation and tell you whether the case makes sense. If we decide to proceed, the initial fee is €190 including VAT and covers the analysis and the report; the 12% applies only to sums actually recovered. If the report says there is nothing to be done, we ask for nothing more.

How long does it take?

The report takes on average ten business days from complete delivery of the documents. The stages that follow — complaint, requests to institutions, a possible lawsuit — are measured in months or years and are not up to us.

Is it true that crypto is unrecoverable?

No, but it is true that often there is nothing left to follow. If the funds reached an exchange that identifies its clients, there is a party to turn to; if they went through a mixer or services with no checks, the trail ends. It is the first thing we verify.

Are you lawyers?

No. We are analysts: we produce technical reports and help prepare the filings. Legal documents are signed by a lawyer — yours, or one of the professionals we work with. We handle the technical side, not representation in court.

Did you call me? I never asked for anything.

We make no cold calls and send no unsolicited messages. If someone presents themselves as working with us without you having contacted us, they do not. Write to assistenza@traccia.it.com and report it.
