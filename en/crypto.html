Crypto scams

Crypto is not anonymous. It is pseudonymous.

Every transaction is written in a public ledger that no one can erase. The problem is not visibility: it is linking an address to a person, or to an entity that actually received the money.

Tracing

Four steps, then the truth.

There is no button that brings crypto back. There is reconstruction work, and in the end an outcome you have to accept.

The question that matters is not «can it be recovered?» but «did the money reach an identifiable party?». Everything else comes after.

  1. 1
    Identify the starting transaction

    The hash, the amount, the date. You need proof that your money went in.

  2. 2
    Follow the subsequent hops

    Intermediary wallets, clusters, conversions between assets, time windows.

  3. 3
    Recognise where the trace stops

    Exchanges with identification, mixers, services without checks: these are different outcomes.

  4. 4
    Build the point of attachment

    Only if there is a party to address can a useful filing be built.

No illusions

Three possible outcomes. One is the most frequent.

Good outcome

Arrival at a KYC exchange

The money stops on a platform that identifies its clients and keeps records. There is a party to send a formal request to, and a documentary trail that can be used.

Uncertain outcome

Wallets still active

The funds are on addresses that have not yet moved. Data preservation can be requested and movements monitored, but there is no telling how long they will stay there.

No route

Mixer or exit from the perimeter

The money passed through obfuscation services or left for jurisdictions that do not cooperate. In these cases we do not take the engagement: it would mean selling you a case with no way forward.

Beware of «recovery agents». Anyone promising to recover crypto with their own tools, to «crack» a wallet or to negotiate with hackers is describing a crime and, almost always, setting up a second fraud. We do none of that.

Why we publish prices

Because some frauds can be recognised from the numbers. If a platform shows you a return of 3 % a month on an asset that has lost 40 % in the meantime, that return does not exist: it is a number drawn to make you deposit more money.

These are real, current market prices. Compare them with the ones you see in the account area of the platform you are considering.

Documents

What you need to start a crypto analysis.

  1. Hashes of the transactions you sent the funds with
  2. Addresses of your wallet and of the one given by the platform
  3. Screenshots of the deposit and withdrawal pages
  4. Statements of the account you started from, with the transfer dates
  5. Conversations with whoever contacted you, with dates and profiles
  6. Any confirmation or «verification» emails received from the platform

We do not need your wallet's seed phrase and must never have it. If the wallet is still under your control, we explain how to secure it without sharing anything.
