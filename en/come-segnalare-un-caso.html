How to report a case, step by step.

Six details, ten minutes, no password. Here is what the form asks for, why it asks, and what we do once you've pressed «Send request».

The same explanation you'll find written below, spoken aloud. Choose the language: the video exists in Italian, English, German and Spanish.

Synthetic voice, not a person. If the video doesn't play, everything it says is written below, in the same words.

The form

Six details, and no password.

Nothing more than what's needed to get back to you and see whether the case falls within what we cover. No data about accounts or credentials.

01

First name

As on your ID. It's the first thing we read.

02

Last name

Separate from the first name: the engagement contract needs them apart.

03

Email

The address we'll reply to. Check it before you send: a typo here means a reply that never arrives.

04

Phone

With the country code. Someone who has just lost money shouldn't wait for an email, and this way we can call you from abroad too.

05

Approximate amount lost

In euro, even roughly. It tells us what kind of case this is.

06

What happened to you

One of the five dropdown options. It tells the analyst which part of the office to open.

The dropdown

Choose the one that fits best.

If you're torn between two, it's not a problem: we'll sort it out on the phone. If you don't know at all, there's «I don't know yet» — it's a real answer, and many people use it.

Fake broker or trading platform

A platform that looked like a financial intermediary and wasn't: you can deposit, you can't withdraw. We ask for the transfers, the receipts and screenshots of the platform's logged-in area.

Cryptocurrency fraud

Money sent in cryptocurrency to a self-styled consultant or a platform. We need the wallet addresses and the transactions: they can be traced from the blockchain.

They asked for money to unlock my withdrawal

The «taxes», the «unlock fees», the «verification»: money asked for to release money that will never come out. It's the second request, and it shouldn't be paid. We need the chats and the receipts for those payments too.

I was contacted by a fake fund recovery service

Someone promising to recover money already lost, asking for an advance. It's a scam inside a scam: don't pay, and keep the messages. Those are what interest us most.

I don't know yet

That's fine. Tell us what happened in the contact email or on the phone: working out where the scam is, is our job, not yours.

Il recupero

How we try to get the money back, after a fraud.

It's the question everyone asks us, and it's fair to answer before you ask: what we do, with what proof, and what we cannot promise.

First step

We trace where the money went

Transfer or payment: the receipt, the date, the beneficiary. Then where it arrived, where it went, and what is left — chats, emails, screenshots.

Sul merito

Sometimes it works, sometimes it doesn't

Sometimes the money is still traceable, or there is an intermediary we can identify. Sometimes there is nothing to be done, and in that case we say so at once, instead of letting you wait.

Il limite

Nobody can promise the result

We can promise the method: technical proof, a clear answer, and no advance to get started. Anyone who guarantees the recovery is asking you for money for something they cannot know.

Plainly

What we will never ask you for.

A password

Not for your banking, not for the platform, not for your email. The form has no password field and never will. If someone claiming to be Traccia asks for a password, it isn't Traccia.

An advance to «unlock» the case

The first assessment is free and we never ask for a payment to get something started. The start-up contribution is paid only once the case has been assessed and you have decided to go ahead, under the terms written in fees.

A promise of recovery

Nobody can promise to bring your money back. We commit to telling you, with technical evidence, where it went and whether there is a workable route. If there isn't, we say so.

After you send

What happens after you press «Send request».

Straight away

The request reaches an analyst

This is not a form that lands in an archive: someone reads it.

Within 48 working hours

We call you back

On the number you gave us, with the country code you wrote. If you don't pick up, we try again.

On the phone

We work out whether the case is ours

A call of about thirty minutes. If the case falls outside what we cover, we tell you straight away and ask you for nothing.

Necessary notice. Traccia carries out technical and documentary analysis and assists in reconstructing financial flows. We are not a lawyer, a law firm, a credit institution, a debt collection company authorised under art. 115 of the Italian Public Security Act (TULPS), nor a financial intermediary. We cannot guarantee that funds will be recovered: the outcome depends on factors beyond our control, such as whether the funds can be traced, cooperation from the institutions involved and the pace of the courts. No operator can promise a guaranteed refund: anyone who does is running a scam within a scam. Our WhatsApp number is only the one published on this site: we never write first and never ask for money in chat.

Anti-fraud. We never ask you to pay money up front to «unlock» a refund, to pay advance taxes, to install remote-access software or to share OTP codes and private keys. Any request of this kind should be reported immediately to assistenza@traccia.it.com.
