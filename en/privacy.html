1. Data controller

The data controller is ERSEL SPA, with registered office at Piazza Solferino, 11 - 10121 Turin (TO), Italy, VAT 06900230018. To exercise your rights you can write to assistenza@traccia.it.com.

2. Data collected

  • Identification data: first name, last name, date of birth, tax code, identity document.
  • Contact data: address, email, phone number.
  • Financial data: transactions, balances, financial instruments held, account details.
  • Browsing data: IP address, device type, pages visited, technical and analytics cookies.

3. Purposes and legal bases

4. Retention period

Data is kept for the whole duration of the contractual relationship and afterwards for the period required by law (generally 10 years for accounting and tax data).

5. Recipients

Data may be shared with partner banks, custodians, technology service providers and advisers, appointed as processors under Art. 28 GDPR. No data is sold to third parties.

6. Your rights

You may exercise at any time the rights set out in Arts. 15–22 GDPR:

  • access to your personal data;
  • rectification or erasure;
  • restriction of and objection to processing;
  • data portability;
  • withdrawal of consent, without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing.

7. Complaints

You may lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (garanteprivacy.it) or with the supervisory authority of your country of residence.

Necessary notice. Traccia carries out technical and documentary analysis and assists in reconstructing financial flows. We are not a lawyer, a law firm, a credit institution, a debt collection company authorised under art. 115 of the Italian Public Security Act (TULPS), nor a financial intermediary. We cannot guarantee that funds will be recovered: the outcome depends on factors beyond our control, such as whether the funds can be traced, cooperation from the institutions involved and the pace of the courts. No operator can promise a guaranteed refund: anyone who does is running a scam within a scam. Our WhatsApp number is only the one published on this site: we never write first and never ask for money in chat.

Anti-fraud. We never ask you to pay money up front to «unlock» a refund, to pay advance taxes, to install remote-access software or to share OTP codes and private keys. Any request of this kind should be reported immediately to assistenza@traccia.it.com.
