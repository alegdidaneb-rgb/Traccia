Organisation

Two people on every case, always.

Every case has a lead analyst and a reviewer who took no part in the analysis. That is why 31 % of cases close at phase 2: when the reviewer finds no point of attachment, the case does not proceed, and is not billed.

No analyst handles more than twelve active cases at once. It is a limit we set ourselves so as not to work on a production line, and it costs us clients.

  • 2019

    The office is founded. Three analysts, one field: fraud on trading platforms with transfers abroad.

  • 2021

    First report filed in court and admitted by the court as a party expert opinion.

  • 2023

    Blockchain intelligence. The on-chain unit is born: stablecoin tracing and reconstruction of clustering wallets.

  • 2025

    Network of correspondents in fourteen jurisdictions, for requests that must be filed abroad.

  • 2026

    Client area. Every reporting party sees the status of their case, the documents and the history of updates.

Collaborations

Who works alongside us.

We stay on the technical side. The filings are signed by those qualified to sign them: that is why we work with a stable network of professionals.

Civil lawyers

Professionals who receive the report and turn it into a filing: formal notices, summonses, appeals. We take no percentage of their fees and do not impose them: you can use your own lawyer.

Foreign correspondents

Funds that have left Italy need requests filed in the right jurisdiction, in the right language. Fourteen countries covered, with verified professionals.

Consumer associations

We work together on group cases and prevention initiatives. If your case follows the same pattern as a hundred others, the collective route is often more effective.

Universities and research

Two applied research projects on wallet-clustering techniques. The results update our protocols, not our brochures.

Standards

How we keep our course straight.

Three internal rules worth more than any certificate, and that anyone can check by talking to us.

  • Every report cites the source of every figure and states the limits of the analysis.
  • No conclusion is written unless the reviewer has checked it.
  • No data of the reporting party is used for purposes other than the case.
  • If a case is not in the reporting party's interest, we say so even when it is in ours.
