Fees

Our limits

The five things we do not negotiate on.

These are the same promises you find on the sites of those who profit from frauds. We write them down so you can compare them with what others tell you.

✕

Guaranteeing recovery

No operator in the world can do that. Anyone writing it is lying and, in most cases, is a fraudster themselves.

✕

Asking for an advance to «unlock» the funds

There is no advance tax to pay to release capital. Our fee is the price of an analysis and does not change with the outcome.

✕

Accessing your device or your online banking

No remote access, no credentials, no seed phrase, no signing of transactions. If some «technician» asks you for that, it is the second fraud.

✕

Involving «hackers» or recovery groups

Recovery obtained by breaking into other people's systems is not lawful recovery, and those who propose it live off that trade. We work only with lawful data and formal requests.

✕

Contacting people who have not written to us

We make no cold calls, do not buy lists of defrauded people, do not send social media messages. You contact us, and only then do we talk.

The contract

The rules in writing, before you sign.

When you sign and what it contains

After the free assessment and before any payment. It sets out the subject of the engagement, the fee, the percentage with its calculation method, withdrawal terms, the privacy notice and the limits of the service.

Can I withdraw?

Yes. You can withdraw before the analysis begins at no cost. After it begins, the initial fee covers the work already done and is not returned; if you withdraw, we stop at once and no further amounts accrue.

Does the percentage last forever?

It applies to recoveries linked to the case for which the engagement was given, within 24 months of signing. It is not an assignment of the claim and does not bind the future management of your assets.

Invoicing and traceability of payments

Every payment is invoiced by ERSEL SPA and must be made only by transfer to the IBAN stated in the contract. We do not accept payments in cryptocurrency, on prepaid cards or to accounts held by anyone other than the company. If someone asks you for this in our name, it is a fraud.

Are there costs I was not expecting?

Not from us. If you decide to incur them, lawyer's fees, court costs, translations and bank charges remain yours. All items you pay directly to the providers and which are listed in the contract.
